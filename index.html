About Us

A small kitchen with big care

I opened Royal Taste five years ago with my brother, in a small corner of Main Market. We had only a few tables and one idea: serve the food we would happily feed our own family.

My mother's sauce recipe is still the base of everything we make. Customers who tried it in our first month still come back, now with their children.

Whether you sit with us, pick up on your way home or order delivery, you get the same plate we would serve at home.

  • Fresh ingredients bought daily, never stored for days
  • Special homemade sauces, made in our own kitchen
  • Affordable prices, with every rate listed openly
  • Family-friendly seating, clean and comfortable
  • Neatly presented food, whether dine-in or takeaway
